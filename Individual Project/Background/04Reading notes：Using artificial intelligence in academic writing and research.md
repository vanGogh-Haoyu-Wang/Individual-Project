# 阅读笔记：Using artificial intelligence in academic writing and research

**文献标题:** Using artificial intelligence in academic writing and research: An essential productivity tool 
**作者:** Mohamed Khalifa, Moataz Albadawy (2024) 
**研究主题:** 探讨 [[Generative AI]]（生成式AI）在学术写作与研究中的应用，强调其作为 [[Productivity Tool]]（生产力工具）的巨大潜力，同时明确界定使用的伦理边界和学术风险。

## 核心观点与发现 (Key Findings)

- **全流程效率提升：** AI 不仅仅用于文本润色，它能够深度参与学术研究的各个阶段，包括头脑风暴、文献检索、大纲构建、草稿生成以及最终的格式编排。
- **克服写作障碍：** 对于科研人员而言，大模型能极大改善学术表达的流畅度，帮助克服“面对空白屏幕”的写作焦虑（Writer's block），从而加速科研成果的产出。
- **[[Hallucination]] (幻觉) 与严谨性风险：** 论文明确指出，LLM 极易捏造虚假的参考文献或编造事实。研究者必须对 AI 生成的所有内容进行严格的事实核查，绝不能盲目轻信。
- **不可替代的 [[Critical Thinking]] (批判性思维)：** AI 缺乏对科学概念的真正理解和逻辑推演能力。数据的解读、实验设计的逻辑以及最终的学术主张，必须由人类研究者绝对主导。
- **[[Ethical AI]] (符合伦理的AI使用)：** 在学术论文中使用 AI 辅助时，必须保持高度的透明度。作者需要对最终成果承担完全的学术责任，并在方法论或致谢部分明确声明所使用的 AI 工具、模型版本及具体用途。

## 对毕设项目的启发 (Inspirations for the MSc Project)

1. **深化 Obsidian 的 AI 插件应用：** 我已经在 Obsidian 中配置了 Copilot 和 Self-hosted LiveSync 等本地组件。根据文献的建议，不仅要用它们来整理前期搭建 SMOP 的技术开发笔记，还可以利用它们在接下来的文献阅读阶段进行学术摘要提取和逻辑梳理，将其真正转化为研究阶段的 [[Productivity Tool]]。
2. **建立严格的“防幻觉”审核流：** 结合之前关于代码翻译风险的阅读，必须警惕双重 [[Hallucination]]——不仅在将 MATLAB 转化为 Python 时要防范算法逻辑错误，在使用大模型辅助撰写项目研究报告时，也要坚决避免大模型编造虚假的参考文献。所有学术主张必须亲自回归原文核实。
3. **提前构思透明度声明：** 项目指南中明确要求遵循伯明翰大学和罗素集团的生成式 AI 使用原则。这篇文献提供了极佳的实践指南：在最终交付的毕设报告中，需要预留一个专门的章节，透明地记录你是如何使用 GPT-4、本地开源模型（如 Qwen 或 Gemma 系列）以及 SMOP 工具的，并声明你作为第一作者对转换后代码的等价性和最终结果负全部责任。
4. **优化开发日志的撰写负荷：** 项目强调“记录所有的提示词、响应和迭代改进”。利用大模型的文本生成能力，可以快速将日常凌乱的代码调试记录（比如前期修复 networkx 依赖冲突的排错过程）转化为结构化、合乎学术规范的英文进展报告。这能大幅释放认知负荷，把核心精力保留在声发射算法的验证上。

# English Translation

**Paper Title:** Using artificial intelligence in academic writing and research: An essential productivity tool
**Authors:** Mohamed Khalifa, Moataz Albadawy (2024)
**Research Topic:** Exploring the application of [[Generative AI]] in academic writing and research, emphasizing its huge potential as a [[Productivity Tool]], while clearly defining the ethical boundaries and academic risks of its use.

## Key Findings

- **Efficiency improvement across the entire process:** AI is not just for text polishing; it can be deeply involved in various stages of academic research, including brainstorming, literature search, outline construction, draft generation, and final formatting.
- **Overcoming writing barriers:** For researchers, large models can greatly improve the fluency of academic expression and help overcome the anxiety of facing a blank screen (Writer's block), thereby accelerating the output of research results.
- **[[Hallucination]] and Rigor Risks:** The paper explicitly points out that LLMs are prone to fabricating fake references or facts. Researchers must conduct strict fact-checking on all content generated by AI and must never trust it blindly.
- **Irreplaceable [[Critical Thinking]]:** AI lacks true understanding of scientific concepts and logical deduction capabilities. The interpretation of data, the logic of experimental design, and the final academic claims must be absolutely dominated by human researchers.
- **[[Ethical AI]] (Ethical use of AI):** High transparency must be maintained when using AI assistance in academic papers. Authors need to take full academic responsibility for the final results and clearly state the AI tools used, model versions, and specific purposes in the methodology or acknowledgments section.

## Inspirations for the MSc Project

1. **Deepen the application of AI plugins in Obsidian:** I have already configured local components such as Copilot and Self-hosted LiveSync in Obsidian. According to the literature's suggestions, these should not only be used to organize technical development notes from setting up SMOP but also leveraged during the upcoming literature reading phase for academic abstract extraction and logical sorting, truly transforming it into a [[Productivity Tool]] for the research phase.
2. **Establish a strict "anti-hallucination" review flow:** Combined with previous readings on code translation risks, one must be wary of double [[Hallucination]]—not only preventing algorithmic logic errors when converting MATLAB to Python but also resolutely avoiding the fabrication of fake references when using large models to assist in writing project research reports. All academic claims must be personally verified against the original text.
3. **Plan the transparency statement in advance:** The project guidelines explicitly require following the generative AI use principles of the University of Birmingham and the Russell Group. This literature provides an excellent practical guide: in the final MSc report, a dedicated section should be reserved to transparently record how GPT-4, local open-source models (such as the Qwen or Gemma series), and the SMOP tool were used, while declaring that you, as the first author, take full responsibility for the equivalence of the converted code and the final results.
4. **Optimize the writing load of development logs:** The project emphasizes "recording all prompts, responses, and iterative improvements." By utilizing the text generation capabilities of large models, messy daily code debugging records (such as the previous troubleshooting process for fixing networkx dependency conflicts) can be quickly transformed into structured, academically standard English progress reports. This can significantly reduce the cognitive load, allowing core energy to be reserved for the verification of acoustic emission algorithms.
